Preparedness Guides

Downloadable guidelines and checklists to organize safety plans across all key areas of life.

Family Preparedness

Ensure your household operates cohesively during grid outages, evacuations, and flash hazards.

Checklist Focus

  • Establish escape exits in every room
  • Designate outer family meeting spot

Safety Tip: Walk children through exit routes quarterly so they don't panic.

Build Family Plan

Workplace Preparedness

Develop continuity protocols, assign safety wardens, and prepare office infrastructure.

Checklist Focus

  • Install lit exit signs and path guides
  • Audit structural first-aid cabinetry

Safety Tip: Back up operational records on secure remote servers daily.

Read Workplace Guide

School Preparedness

Ensure student security through teacher responsibilities and safe lockdown protocols.

Checklist Focus

  • Maintain child release authority lists
  • Conduct monthly fire & storm drills

Safety Tip: Keep emergency food pouches stored in gym lockers.

Read School Guide

Community Preparedness

Organize CERT networks, map sandbag lockers, and identify vulnerable households.

Checklist Focus

  • Compile phone logs of elderly neighbors
  • Stockpile communal cleaning supplies

Safety Tip: Share sandbags or snow blower gear with nearby residents.

Join Local CERT

Emergency Communication

Set check-in nodes to coordinate group status updates when cell services degrade.

Checklist Focus

  • Assign out-of-town contact person
  • Download emergency radio applications

Safety Tip: Rely on SMS text messages over phone calls to save bandwidth.

Read Comm Guide

Evacuation Planning

Determine safe road routes and packing checklists to exit high-risk areas quickly.

Checklist Focus

  • Map evacuation routes out of town
  • Keep vehicle fuel tanks at least half-full

Safety Tip: Keep physical road maps inside the vehicle in case GPS fails.

Read Evac Guide

Emergency Kit Preparation

Pack a modular, high-portability survival bag containing three days of food and water.

Checklist Focus

  • Water (one gallon per person per day)
  • First-aid kit & emergency flashlight

Safety Tip: Review expiration dates on supplies twice a year.

Build Survival Bag

Landslide Preparedness

Understand soil drainage paths and secure buildings on steep terrain slope lines.

Checklist Focus

  • Inspect slope retaining walls
  • Monitor tilting trees or fence posts

Safety Tip: Keep hillsides clear of unanchored soil caches.

Read Landslide Guide

Winter & Cold Safety

Insulate utility water lines and prepare heating reserves to survive sub-zero hazards.

Checklist Focus

  • Wrap exposed copper water pipes
  • Verify backup generator fuel reserves

Safety Tip: Never operate combustion heaters indoors without vents.

Read Winter Guide
Crisis Management

Integrated Responder Wardens

In high-urgency storm, flood, or fire warnings, municipal response assets require immediate roadway routes. SafeAlert is engineered to help coordinate localized emergency logistics so that rescue crews can deploy swiftly.

Practicing family evacuation drills minimizes delays, leaving city roads clear for heavy response vehicles to reach high-priority emergency alerts.

Speed Matters

The Crucial Benefit of Evacuation Drills

Simulation exercises transform chaotic crisis scenarios into calm, reflex-driven evacuations. Families that map out routes and practice them exit hazard zones four times faster than families without a plan.

Practicing evacuation routes ensures that power blackouts, sudden smoke hazards, or blocked passages do not lead to panic or delays.
