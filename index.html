Essential Supplies

Build Your Emergency Kit

A standard 72-hour survival kit contains these ten key categories. Do you have them ready?

Water

1 Gallon per person per day for drinking & sanitation.

Food

High protein canned or freeze-dried meals.

First Aid

Bandages, antiseptic wipes, scissors, and guide book.

Flashlight

Tactical LED light to navigate in total darkness.

Radio

Hand-crank or NOAA weather alert radio updates.

Batteries

Fresh backup supply of standard AA and AAA cells.

Power Bank

High-capacity battery for charging devices.

Whistle

A reliable tool to signal emergency rescue teams.

Blanket

Mylar solar blankets to prevent hypothermia.

Documents

Waterproof container with vital personal paperwork.

4-Step Action Path

How To Prepare

Learn the chronological milestones to turn awareness into actionable emergency resilience.

1

Know the Risk

Conduct research on natural disaster records in your region. Find out if your residence sits in a low-lying flood zone or adjacent to dry woodland.

2

Make a Plan

Define two exit escape points per room, mapping exact paths to family meeting spots outside risk zones. Establish a designated emergency communication protocol.

3

Build a Kit

Assemble waterproof containers filled with water, stable shelf food, emergency lighting tools, battery packs, and dynamic medicine reserves.

4

Stay Informed

Download emergency safety warning apps, acquire an automated NOAA weather radio unit, and actively monitor municipal notifications.

Immediate Response Advice

Disaster Safety Tips

Crucial guidelines to protect your health and survival when emergencies strike.

Flood Safety

Relocate immediately to higher elevations when flash flood warnings display.

Keep your emergency supplies accessible and sealed in watertight dry-packs.

Earthquake Safety

Practice Drop, Cover, and Hold On under heavy wood structures.

Stay clear of windows, heavy shelves, glass panels, and brick walls.
